# FacultyConnect — questions for the professor

Items marked **💲** may cost money and need your approval before we sign up.

## Sign-in (Okta)

The university never shares anyone's password with us. With single sign-on, people log in on the university's own page, and we only receive a confirmation of who they are.

1. Should we use **the university's existing Okta**, or **create our own Okta setup** for the platform?
   - University's Okta: usually free to us, but university IT must approve it, and it only works for that university's people.
   - Our own Okta: works for anyone. 💲 There is a free tier, but connecting a university's login is a paid feature.
2. How will **students from other universities** register and sign in?
3. How should **registration, email verification and password recovery** work? If we use Okta, Okta can handle these.
4. Should registration be **limited to approved university email domains**, for example `.edu` only?

## Zoom

5. Will professors connect their **personal or university Zoom account**? Each professor signs in on Zoom's own page once, and we never see their password.
   - Free Zoom allows meetings up to 40 minutes, so our 30-minute meetings work on free accounts.
   - 💲 Automatic recordings and transcripts need a paid Zoom plan (roughly $13–16 per professor per month).

## Google Calendar

6. Is **Google Calendar** enough, or do some professors also use **Outlook**?
   - The Google Calendar connection is free. A paid Google Workspace subscription is not required.
